Who This Is For

You are planning a renovation.
Something feels off.

Most homeowners arrive at the same place. The quotes are in. The builder seems fine. The design is done. But there is a quiet unease. That feeling is usually right.

You are spending serious money. A £100k to £500k project. The idea of overpaying, or getting it wrong, is keeping you up at night. You need someone who has seen what can go wrong and knows how to prevent it.

You do not know what you do not know. Architects, designers, and builders all speak from their own corner. Nobody is giving you the full picture. You want an independent voice with no agenda.

You want control of the process. You are not looking to hand everything over. You want to understand what is happening, ask the right questions, and make confident decisions at every stage.

Your project is in London. West and South West London renovations come with their own rules, planning pressures, and contractor dynamics. Local knowledge matters more than most people realise.

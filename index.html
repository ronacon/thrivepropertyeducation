Who This Is For

You are planning a renovation. Something feels off.

Most homeowners arrive at the same place. The quotes are in. The builder seems fine. The design is done. But there is a quiet unease. A sense that something important has not been figured out yet. That feeling is usually right. This is for the homeowner who wants to get it right before the money starts moving.

You are spending serious moneyYou have a £100k to £500k project and the idea of overpaying, or getting it wrong, is keeping you up at night. You need someone who has seen what can go wrong and knows how to prevent it.

You do not know what you do not knowArchitects, designers, and builders all speak from their own corner of the project. Nobody is giving you the full picture. You want an independent voice with no agenda.
